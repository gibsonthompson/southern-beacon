Mold Testing & Air Sampling

Independent lab air and surface sampling when you need to know for certain, compared against an outdoor baseline. Available on its own or before and after remediation.

Know For Certain What You Have

You cannot identify a mold species by looking at it, and you cannot see moisture moving inside a wall. Testing takes the guesswork out. Whether you have spotted growth, noticed a persistent musty smell, or want to confirm a space is clean after a water loss, testing tells you what is actually there.

Our testing combines a visual assessment with air and surface sampling, all analyzed by an independent accredited laboratory. You get a written report explaining the findings in plain language, and the report belongs to you to use however you need.

What Testing Includes

  • Visual assessment of the areas of concern and the surrounding spaces
  • Air samples that compare indoor spore levels to an outdoor baseline taken the same day
  • Surface samples of visible growth for identification
  • Analysis by an independent accredited laboratory, never an in-house reading
  • A written report with the findings and clear recommendations

When Testing Makes Sense

Testing is most useful when mold is suspected but not visible, when you need to confirm a space is safe after water damage, during a real estate transaction, or after remediation to verify the work succeeded. If mold is clearly visible and the source is obvious, we will tell you honestly that your money is better spent on the fix than on confirming what you already know.

Independent Results You Can Trust

Every sample goes to an accredited outside lab with no stake in the outcome. If your results come back normal, we tell you that and you are done. We would rather give you an honest answer than sell you work you do not need.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does mold testing cost?+
It depends on how many samples the situation calls for. We quote the number of samples and the price upfront before collecting anything, so there is never a surprise lab bill.
How long do results take?+
Standard laboratory turnaround is two to three business days. Expedited analysis is available when a real estate closing or a health concern will not wait.
What if the test finds mold?+
You get a written report along with your results, and we can walk you through remediation options. There is no obligation, and the report is yours to take to any contractor.
Should I test before or after remediation?+
Both can make sense. Testing before confirms what you are dealing with, and testing after verifies the work returned the space to normal. We can do either or both.
