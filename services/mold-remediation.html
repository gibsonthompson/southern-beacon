Remediation Means Removal, Not Just Spraying

Finding mold is unsettling, and the internet is full of conflicting advice. Here is the straight version: small surface mold on a hard, non-porous surface can sometimes be cleaned by a homeowner, but growth that covers a larger area, keeps coming back, or has gotten into drywall, insulation, or framing needs professional remediation. Spraying a store-bought product over it does not fix it. The material that fed it is still there, and dead spores still cause problems.

We provide complete mold remediation for homes and businesses across metro Atlanta. Every project runs from proper containment through removal to correcting the moisture that caused it, because removing mold without fixing the water just schedules the next outbreak.

What a Project Includes

  • Containment that seals the work area off from the rest of your home
  • Negative air pressure and HEPA filtration so spores do not spread during the work
  • Physical removal of contaminated porous materials, cut back to sound material
  • HEPA vacuuming and detailed cleaning of framing and hard surfaces
  • Finding and correcting the moisture source that made it possible
  • Drying verified with moisture meters, and testing available to confirm the result

Why the Moisture Source Matters

Mold cannot grow without moisture. Every remediation we do includes finding where the water came from, whether it is a plumbing leak, a roof intrusion, foundation seepage, or condensation from poor ventilation. Because we also handle water damage and waterproofing, we can correct the underlying problem on the same project instead of handing you off to another contractor.

Verified, Not Just Finished

A clean-looking room is not the same as a job done right. When it matters, for your peace of mind, an insurance claim, or a home sale, we can test the air after remediation and compare it against an outdoor baseline through an independent lab, so you have documentation that the space is actually back to normal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to move out during remediation?+
Usually not. Containment and negative air isolate the work area from the rest of the home. For extensive contamination, work involving the HVAC system, or households with significant respiratory sensitivities, we will talk through whether relocating for part of the project makes sense.
How long does mold remediation take?+
Small, contained projects are often one to two days. Larger jobs involving demolition and structural drying can take several days to a week. You get a clear timeline with your written estimate.
Can you just spray something to kill it?+
No, and any company offering that is selling a cosmetic result. Killing mold does not remove the allergenic material or the surface it grew on. Physical removal is the right way to do it.
Will insurance cover mold?+
Sometimes. Mold from a sudden covered event like a burst pipe is often covered, while mold from long-term humidity or an unaddressed leak usually is not. We document the cause thoroughly to support your claim.
