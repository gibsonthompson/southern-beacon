Stop the Water at the Source

Metro Atlanta sits on dense red clay that absorbs water slowly and holds it against your foundation. After heavy rain, that saturated clay creates pressure that pushes moisture through block walls, floor joints, and cracks. The result is the damp line, the puddle, or the musty basement smell that comes back every time it storms.

Sealing a crack or rolling on waterproof paint treats the symptom. A properly designed system manages the water itself, collecting it where it enters and moving it away from your foundation for good.

Our Waterproofing Solutions

  • Interior drainage systems that collect water at the footing and channel it to a sump
  • Sump pump installation, including battery backup for storms that knock out power
  • Foundation wall sealing and vapor barriers
  • Crack repair and evaluation of the foundation
  • Exterior grading and downspout drainage correction, often the cheapest and most overlooked fix
  • A written warranty on installed systems

Designed Around Your Foundation

Every home takes on water differently. Before we recommend anything we look at where water is actually entering, how your lot sheds runoff, and what the walls are telling us. The estimate explains what each component does and why it is included, so you are never paying for a package that includes work your house does not need.

Waterproofing and Mold, Handled Together

A basement that has been damp for years almost always has mold to deal with too. Because we handle water damage, mold, and waterproofing under one roof, we can address existing growth and the water source in a single coordinated project. One company, one plan, one warranty.
