When Water Gets In, Every Hour Counts

A burst pipe, a failed water heater, an overflowing appliance, or storm flooding puts water into materials that were never meant to be wet. Drywall wicks it upward within hours. Wood framing and subfloor soak it up. And in Georgia's climate, mold can begin growing within 24 to 48 hours. The faster water is extracted and drying starts, the less damage your home takes and the less the whole thing costs.

We answer the phone 24 hours a day, and emergency calls across metro Atlanta are dispatched immediately. You are not leaving a voicemail and waiting for a callback while water spreads.

What We Do On Site

  • Find and stop the water source, then extract standing water with truck-mounted and portable equipment
  • Remove unsalvageable wet materials (soaked carpet pad, swollen baseboard, saturated drywall) so drying can actually work
  • Set commercial air movers and dehumidifiers sized to the space
  • Log moisture readings daily and keep drying until the structure reads dry, not just feels dry
  • Treat for mold where water sat long enough to start growth
  • Document everything from the first minute for your insurance claim

We Bill Your Insurance Directly

Water damage claims go smoother with thorough documentation, and that is exactly how we work. We photograph conditions from the moment we arrive, log every moisture reading, and build the file from the same estimating platform adjusters use. Then we bill your insurance company directly. You handle your deductible, we handle the rest. If your loss may be covered, our documentation gives your claim the strongest footing.

Dry Means Verified Dry

Surfaces can feel dry to the hand while the framing behind them still holds enough water to grow mold and warp materials. We monitor with moisture meters throughout the drying process and do not pull the equipment until readings confirm the structure has actually returned to normal. You get the drying logs as part of your file.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you get here?+
Emergency water damage calls across metro Atlanta are dispatched immediately, 24 hours a day, including weekends and holidays. The sooner we start extracting and drying, the less damage your home takes.
Should I do anything before you arrive?+
If it is safe, shut off the water source and cut power to affected areas, then move valuables out of the water. Leave extraction and drying to us, because consumer shop vacs and household fans cannot dry structural materials adequately.
Will insurance cover it?+
Sudden, accidental losses like a burst pipe or failed water heater are usually covered. Long-term seepage and neglected maintenance often are not. We document thoroughly and bill your insurance directly, so your out-of-pocket is typically just your deductible.
How long until my home is dry?+
Most residential losses dry in three to five days, depending on how much water there was and which materials got wet. We log moisture daily and keep going until it reads dry.
