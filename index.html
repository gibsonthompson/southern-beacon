FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you get here after water damage?+
We answer 24 hours a day and dispatch emergency water damage calls immediately. In water losses the first hours matter most, because drywall wicks moisture and mold can start within 24 to 48 hours, so we get a crew moving as fast as possible.
Do you bill insurance directly?+
Yes. We document the loss with photos and moisture readings, work from the same estimating platform adjusters use, and bill your insurance company directly so you are not floating the cost. You handle your deductible and we handle the rest of the paperwork.
Will my water damage be covered by insurance?+
It depends on the cause. Sudden, accidental events like a burst pipe, a failed water heater, or a supply line break are usually covered. Long-term seepage and unaddressed maintenance issues often are not. We document everything thoroughly so your claim has the strongest possible support.
How long does drying take?+
Most residential water losses dry in three to five days with commercial air movers and dehumidifiers running, though it depends on how much water there was and which materials got wet. We monitor with moisture meters daily and do not pull equipment until the structure reads dry.
Do I have mold, or just water damage?+
Any material that stays wet long enough will grow mold, often within a couple of days. If water sat for a while before we arrived, or if there is a musty smell, we look for mold and can test to confirm. Fast drying is the best way to prevent it in the first place.
What does water damage restoration cost?+
It depends on the size of the loss, how many rooms are affected, and how much material has to be removed. When it is an insurance claim, your out-of-pocket is typically just your deductible. For non-covered work you get a written estimate before anything starts.
Can you handle both the water and the mold?+
Yes. We handle water extraction and drying, mold remediation, and the waterproofing that stops it from happening again, all in house. One company, one plan, no finger-pointing between contractors about whose scope a problem belonged to.
Are you available on weekends and holidays?+
Yes. Water does not wait for business hours. We answer the phone and dispatch crews 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and holidays.
